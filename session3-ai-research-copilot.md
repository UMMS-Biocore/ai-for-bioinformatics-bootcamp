## The four AI-use modes (task-level rules)

> _Organizing frame (grounded in Zyphur/Instats — see README). Each mode gets one rule + a demo._
- **Search** — verify every citation against the primary source before it enters your work.
- **Co-author** — allowed on text *you* could produce (polish, register, length); not to generate content you couldn't produce yourself. Disclose per the target venue.
- **Validator** — treat AI critique as a brainstorm prompt, not as validation; critique with a *different* model family (see below).
- **Tutor** — prefer corpus-grounded tutoring (your reading list / a knowledge base) over free-form chat; demonstrate independent recall before counting it as learning.

## Prompting craft

> _TODO: teach the recipe + let them practice._
- **Structure:** context + task + constraints + output spec. Assign a role.
- **Model-to-task matching:** brainstorming vs. long-document analysis vs. literature search.
- **Grounding:** give it the source (paste the paper/data) rather than trusting free recall.
- **Iterate:** refine the prompt, keep a human in the loop.

## Verify-everything (carry-over from Session 1)

> _TODO: a short checklist learners apply to every AI output before it enters their work._
- **Model-heterogeneity** — validate an argument with a *different* model family than the one that produced it; the same model can't be both author and independent critic.
- **Sycophancy check** — agreement is not validation; flat agreement with your framing is a red flag.
- **Disclosure template (deliverable)** — a reusable stub: tool + version, use-mode(s), scope, verification steps, model/temperature/seed, limitations.
